I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at **Virginia Tech**, focusing on wireless communications. My research interests include **massive random access**, **Age of Information (AoI) minimization**, and learning-based optimization of next-generation networks.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Network Engineer at **Ericsson** in Nanjing. I received my M.Sc. in Communication & Information Systems from **Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications** (2025) and my B.Eng. in Electronics & Information Engineering from **Nantong University** (2022).

Outside of the terminal, I spend my downtime optimizing culinary techniques and practicing classical vocal music.
